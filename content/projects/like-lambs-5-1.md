In the November 2 group exercise, use a short passage from *Like Lambs* to practice channel-based surround placement in REAPER. This also introduces the supplied film material as an optional source for [Project 4: Atmos Mix](/projects/atmos/). No separate full-film mix is required for this exercise.

For optional extended practice, create a 5.1 mix of the film Like Lambs. I have given you all of the sound effects and dialogue. You need to first set appropriate levels, then pan them around the space using either the _Channel Mapper-Downmixer_ or _ReaSurroundPan_. If you want the sound to only come out of just one speaker, out of a set of speakers, or all the speakers at once use the _Channel Mapper-Downmixer_. If you have a sound that you want to place in one spot in the room (point source), use the _ReaSurroundPan_. A successful project should show examples of both cases.

Include at least two panning automation moves. Try to record the automation with write mode.

Remember what we learned from watching many movies 5.1. This format provides a very large dynamic range, use it. Have quiet sounds and loud sounds for contrast. Spatial separation can help distinguish sources, but masking still needs listening checks. Be careful as you mix to not have effects covering each other.

We watched many examples of panning and dialogue. Dialogue is usually placed in the front speakers but can be placed in the surrounds and even panned for dramatic effect. Should your soundscape be natural or unnatural? How will you use the sub?

For the class exercise, demonstrate your placement and automation choices in the shared session. Participation is assessed complete/incomplete; there is no separate file submission. If you develop the material for the Atmos project, follow that project's duration, Logic session, ADM, and QC requirements.
