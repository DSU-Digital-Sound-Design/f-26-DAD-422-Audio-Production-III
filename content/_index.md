This course introduces the principles and practices of spatial audio and
immersive sound design through a hands-on, project-based approach. It begins
with acoustics and the physical properties of sound, then works through stereo,
binaural, ambisonic, and multichannel formats using both historical and
contemporary spatial audio technologies.

Alongside technical instruction in recording, mixing, and software such as
REAPER and the Ambisonic Toolkit, the course builds critical listening,
collaborative critique, and an awareness of spatial aesthetics in music, sound
art, and cinema. Through progressively more complex projects — mono sound
design, stereo and binaural recording, ambisonic mixing, and Dolby Atmos
production — you develop the technical fluency and creative judgement to build
convincing multidimensional soundscapes.
